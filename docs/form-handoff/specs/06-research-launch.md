# S06: research baseline, quality gates and release evidence

## One reconciled dataset plan

Registry has30 authorized-photo slots, not30 invented photos. Keep actual photos, signed permissions and identifiable vehicle information in an approved private location. Public repository contains synthetic fixtures and IDs only. Register rights, permitted uses, make/body category, visible features, angle/light/occlusion and duplication/overlap. Unknown authorization blocks real processing and marketing.

18 development photos x3 tasks x3 configurations =162 outputs. Six of those cars x3 sequential edits x3 configurations =54 more. This preserves the original216-output development proposal. The remaining12 photos are held out; after choosing a configuration,12 x3 tasks x1 final configuration =36 verification outputs. Total252 is a proposed two-stage plan, NOT an authorization or completed result. Any quality-gate re-test after changing prompts/model must identify reused holdout contamination or use fresh held-out data.

Three tasks: controlled color-only edit, a full supported style, then a single wheel/detail edit. Sequential assessment records drift from both immediate base and original. Score identity, target execution, protected parts, artifacts and willingness to retain separately. Invisible badges/steering/part numbers are N/A, not inferred. Preserve the first output and every failure/cancel/retry in denominators. Mode=demo/mock does not enter live quality results. A generic editor is a useful baseline only when actually operated with the same photo/task under a separately approved budget.

Proposed internal release gate, not a market benchmark: zero unresolved identity-critical failures in the locked verification set; at least90% judged usable across assessed requested outputs; non-delivered outputs count in total delivery reporting. Publish per-task/per-vehicle subgroup counts and uncertainty, not just aggregate percentage. This small set cannot prove all vehicles supported. If a capability misses the gate, keep it disabled or narrow supported scope; do not remove its failures from the report. No attempt to use a single AI judge as absolute truth.

## Human research

Round1:8-12 recruited target users interact with clearly labelled demo to assess understanding, first action, consent and feedback.30 seconds is an observation checkpoint, not a claim that real generation completes then. Do not infer live fidelity or willingness to pay from deterministic sample output.

Round2:12-16 car owners use actual authorized results after budget/model ready. Observe independent completion, whether it is still their car, purposeful refinement and actual favorite/export choices. Record prior participation/learning effect and recruitment bias; these small qualitative groups do not estimate national conversion or market share. Invitees consent to study recording; no private notes posted to public PR.

Metrics: first valid result and first usable result separately; time from selected photo to usable result; provider attempts per accepted job; explicit favorite/export; voluntary new exploration distinct from corrective retry; completed payment separate from opened paywall; share sheet open separate from actual platform outcome. Money includes free users, failures, refunds, compensations, abandoned jobs and unresolved supplier exposure.

## Pricing experiment (after reliable results)

Proposed one-off5/15 credit packs are a simple catalog structure, not approved prices. Test controlled real trial versus a transparent pay-before-generate path only after budget/anti-abuse and unit cost are known. Keep photos/cohort/model/pack price as comparable as possible; don't change five variables and attribute gains to trial. Trial default remains disabled. Do not import a competitor's coin count as an equal image count. Do not claim a national CPI or price elasticity without measured data.

## Fourteen independent release gates

G01 Scope/contracts: approved features and exact build/config frozen; unsupported promises absent.
G02 Migration/recovery: original-data reconciliation, backup and restore rehearsal passed.
G03 Identity/access: ownership, auth/signature/challenge, merge/session/cross-user isolation independently reviewed.
G04 Submission/ledger: races, idempotency and exact user-lot accounting verified.
G05 Worker/budget: restart, unknown supplier cost, late result and circuit breakers verified.
G06 Live quality: authorized development/held-out results, model/profile/prompt versions and capability decisions recorded.
G07 Native: actual iPhone import/HEIC/permissions/background/export/accessibility and supportedOS evidence.
G08 IAP/refunds: genuine StoreKit/Sandbox/end-to-end server verification and out-of-order refund tests, correct catalog.
G09 Privacy/deletion: actual data map/retention/processors/consent, deletion and backup replay verified.
G10 Assets/brand: production images/fonts/icons/marks licensed; unsupported registration mark removed.
G11 Storefront/entity: approved countries, correct local products, tax/bank/agreements and relevant legal review.
G12 Economics/trial: supplier cost bounds, free-user exposure, support/refund and marketing budgets approved.
G13 Operations/support: alerting, owner, incident pause, support and reconciliation runbooks exercised.
G14 Independent/owner approval: named independent reviewer and explicit owner go/no-go for exact candidate build.

Each gate reports evidence kind, codeCommit, status VERIFIED/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN, reviewer, source/evidence reference and SHA256 for public redacted evidence. A manual legal note cannot substitute for an iPhone test, and a pure unit suite cannot substitute for live model quality. check_release.py verifies formatting/existence/hashes and exact gate coverage, NOT truth, law, actual signature provenance or authorization. Successful output is EVIDENCE_COMPLETE_PENDING_OWNER_ACTION, never a deployment command.

## Shipping and incident controls

No build is uploaded, listing submitted, country enabled, merge performed or production data touched by this handoff. App Store review requires actual accessible functionality; don't ship a demo-only paid wrapper. Keep billing/generation disabled when prerequisites absent. TestFlight is controlled testing, not a profitable market test by itself. Release re-check current Apple SDK, account deletion, privacy, IAP and country rules. During incident pause new spending first; keep lawful history/export available, explain status and reconcile existing credits.
