# F30 owner storefront checklist

US is proposed first public availability; approvedMarkets remains empty and all
release/device/IAP/quality/privacy/entity gates false. This task changes no App
Store availability, product IDs, agreement, bank/tax fact or deployed service.
AU, GB, NZ and future storefronts require separate approval and scoped evidence.

Use actual StoreKit countryCode (USA/AUS/GBR etc.), normalized to internal alpha2.
Device locale/IP/GPS is not a storefront or legal-residence substitute. Unknown or
unapproved storefront disables new sales; preserve existing owned history/export
and ledger access. Backend authorization still verifies the actor independently.
StoreKit displayPrice remains the exact localized source; this policy formats no
currency and never estimates a price from the country.

Before activating an actual candidate, record privately:

- Exact candidate build, owner approval, independent security and payment review.
- App/bundle/team and signed Paid Applications agreement, product IDs/types/units.
- App and IAP country availability, actual prices, no automatic future expansion.
- Actual publisher/export eligibility and tax/bank/contract review references.
- Applicable privacy/consumer review, real support/refund/deletion routing.
- Licensed local-car quality and supplier/hosting/retention coverage evidence.
- Actual iPhone/StoreKit/Sandbox/transaction recovery and unknown/travel cases.

All activation and real device/platform rows are NOT_RUN. F32 must consume approved
private facts; setting a client flag never grants credits or authorizes spending.
No recommendation here claims national profit/CPI or completes a legal review.

Official country-code reference:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/storefront/countrycode
